Team comparison

This tool helps see how your Umamusume teams stack up against each other. Each team is tested against opponents drawn from a representative pool of teams, so the result reflects how it performs across the whole field.
Every trial picks a race seed, 2 opponent teams and a gate order, then runs each of your teams under exactly those conditions, so any difference comes down to the teams themselves.
